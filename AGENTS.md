# Agent Autopilot

For non-trivial work, use the ultimate-agentic-workflow rule.

Classify work by tier:
- T0: answer or tiny safe edit directly.
- T1: short approach, execute, verify.
- T2: spec and plan before implementation.
- T3: full traceability workflow.

Before implementing a .workflow task, consult `.workflow/_dream/playbook.digest.md` (compact curated gotchas/rules; advisory, below docs/spec). Open `.workflow/_dream/playbook.md` for an entry's full text, provenance, and superseded history.

Do not claim completion without fresh evidence.
Keep always-loaded instructions short.
