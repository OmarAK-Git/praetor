What happens

Praetor does not invent the edit. Humans draft the org-policy change from confirmed decisions and analyst notes; Praetor packages that draft as a review-only artifact.

Setup

Why it matters

The draft is refused for activation on purpose. A SOC lead must promote it in a separate workflow before it becomes real policy.
